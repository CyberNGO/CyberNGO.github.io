About Bhaumik

Bhaumik Merchant is a cybersecurity professional, ethical hacker, and the visionary founder of CyberNGO. With a deep passion for digital safety and community empowerment, he started CyberNGO to bridge the gap between technology and society.

He believes that cybersecurity is not just for experts — it’s for everyone. Through CyberNGO, he leads a movement to educate, empower, and protect individuals and organizations against evolving cyber threats.

His leadership is rooted in values of integrity, innovation, and impact. Under his guidance, CyberNGO has grown into a trusted name in cybersecurity awareness, capacity building, and social impact.

His Journey

  1. The Beginning Started exploring cybersecurity out of curiosity and a desire to make the digital world safer.
  2. The Realization Recognized the lack of awareness and the urgent need for accessible cybersecurity education.
  3. The Mission Founded CyberNGO to empower individuals, educate communities, and build a safer digital ecosystem.
  4. The Growth Through dedication and teamwork, CyberNGO reached thousands across borders.
  5. The Vision A future where every individual is cyber aware, cyber safe, and cyber strong.

About Our NGO

CyberNGO helps people fight against Cyber Crime and understand the pain and concern of Cyber-crime victims. We help and guide people to fight against Cyber Crime — because being a victim of crime and fighting against it is not easy.
