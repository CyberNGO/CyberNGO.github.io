Why we exist

The problem we're solving

In the digital day and age, from morning till evening we use a variety of tech gadgets and platforms which directly or indirectly affect our living and thought process. As a coin has two sides, growth in technology has drastically increased cyber-crimes.

It is nearly impossible for an individual user to understand the full technology background and its risks, given how much exposure each person has to fancy new technologies. When such an individual falls into a cyber-crime trap, common questions go unanswered.

CyberNGO helps individuals navigate exactly these questions — by updating people on emerging cyber-crime trends, mentoring victims when a crime has already happened, and assisting law-enforcement agencies on demand.

What we do

The DNA of CyberNGO

  1. 01

    Spreading Awareness

    CyberNGO keeps Hawkeye over Global Cyber Threats and emerging Cyber-Scam patterns. We frequently publish Cyber Precaution Guidelines (CPG) so individuals can stay Cyber Smart and counter modern Cyber Attacks.

    • Cyber threat awareness campaigns
    • Workshops and seminars
    • Digital safety guides & resources
    • Community outreach initiatives
  2. 02

    Mentoring Victims

    Our highly technical Cyber Volunteers consult Cyber-Crime victims, make them aware of the Cyber Misery they're facing, and guide them through next steps to get Cyber Justice — using years of law-enforcement experience.

    • One-on-one victim counselling
    • Guidance on legal rights & options
    • Emotional support & recovery
    • Continuous follow-up & mentoring
  3. 03

    Law-Enforcement Support

    Most victims have common questions: "Where do I register a complaint?", "What goes in the incident report?". On-demand, our industry-expert volunteers help law-enforcement agencies investigate Cyber-Crime cases.

    • Technical support & evidence analysis
    • Case documentation assistance
    • Capacity building for agencies
    • Collaboration for stronger action

Our story

About our journey

CyberNGO — a nonprofit Cyber Safety Initiative — is a vision of spreading and creating Cyber Awareness among mankind, and assisting Cyber-Crime victims in achieving Cyber Justice. We started our journey by helping our friends, relatives, and other people around us who had been victims of a Cyber Crime. We spread Cyber Awareness among them and helped with their queries.

We are also associated with Law-Enforcement departments and provide voluntary service by helping them with Cyber Crime cases. During our journey with law enforcement we observed the rapidly increasing scale of Cyber Crime around the country and the world — the spark that ignited the CyberNGO concept.

We observed that in most Cyber-Crime cases, victims simply lacked Cyber Awareness. CyberNGO was developed based on the Digital Movement underway in our nation: while the world advances into a Digital era, it is now a necessity to be aware of the frauds and crimes that take advantage of it. A person has to be aware of Cyber Threats to avoid becoming a victim of one.

Our motto: "Be Aware, Be Safe."
