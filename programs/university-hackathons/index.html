Why we do this

India produces world class cyber security talent that often goes unfocused — bright students with no exposure to adversarial challenges, real world targets, or active mentorship. A 4–8 hour hackathon does what textbook learning can't: it builds the muscle memory, instinct, and community that real cyber-defence requires.

  • Bright students
  • Real world targets
  • Active mentorship
  • Community impact

How it works

What happens in a session

We don't lecture. Every session dives into live challenges that simulate real attacks and real investigations in real time.

  1. 01

    CTF Challenges

    Web, crypto, forensics, reverse engineering tasks

  2. 02

    Live Target Hacking

    Enumerate, exploit, and pivot on live vulnerable systems

  3. 03

    Vulnerability Research

    Find & report real vulnerabilities

  4. 04

    Bug Bounty Workflow

    Learn triage, reproducible disclosure & impact scoring

  5. 05

    Mentor Network

    Direct access to active CyberNGO volunteers

  6. 06

    Leadership Pathway

    Top performers invited to cyber communities

  7. 07

    Hands-on Experience

    Build portfolio & confidence

  8. 08

    Practical Community

    Access global cyber security resources
