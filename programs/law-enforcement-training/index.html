Why this exists

Cyber-crime cases overwhelm units without specialised training. CDR/SDR analysis, evidence chain-of-custody for digital artifacts, and scam-pattern recognition aren't part of standard police curriculum — yet most modern cases hinge on them. CyberNGO has built training rooted in 10+ years of active case work alongside Gujarat Police, ATS, and Crime Branch.

  • Digital Forensics
  • Network & Log Analysis
  • Malware Analysis & Reverse Engineering
  • Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)
  • Investigation Workflow
  • Case Correlation & Timeline
  • Evidence Management
  • Legal & Procedural Awareness

In a session

What happens in a session

We don't lecture. Every session splits into live, hands-on exercises and a take-home utility pack participants can apply the next day.
