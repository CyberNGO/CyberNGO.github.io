Program 02 SCHOOLS

Kids Cyber Ninja Program

Make cyber-safety fun for Class 5–12 — games, not lectures.

  • Schools · Class 5–12 students
  • 1 day · 2 sessions
  • Free of cost
Schedule a session
For
Schools · Class 5–12 students
Duration
1 day · 2 sessions
Format
Auditorium + classroom drill, on-school-premises
Reach
Full grade cohorts (50–300+ students)
Cost
Free of cost

Why this exists

Kids are the first targets of modern online scams — gaming-account theft, fake influencer DMs, OTP-sharing pressure from 'cousins' on WhatsApp. They're the first generation growing up with smartphones from age 6, and they're learning safety by accident, not design. We meet them where they are: in school, with games, not lectures.

  • Fun activities not lectures
  • Age-appropriate content
  • Real-life scenarios
  • Practical skills for everyday safety

How it runs

What happens in a session

  1. 01

    Spot-a-scam workshop

    Students dissect real scam screenshots.

  2. 02

    Password strength challenge

    Gamified, leaderboard-style.

  3. 03

    Privacy-settings walkthrough

    Across Instagram, Snapchat, Discord, Roblox.

  4. 04

    OTP-pressure roleplay

    Practising "no" to authority-figure callers.

  5. 05

    Parent-child cyber-pact template

    Printable, signed by both.

  6. 06

    Age-appropriate apps guide

    For parents.

  7. 07

    Class poster pack

    Visual rules of safe internet use.

  8. 08

    Teacher follow-up kit

    5-minute reinforcement scripts.

Next step

Bring it to your school

  1. 01 Say hello

    Two minutes on the form below. Tell us your school, your classes, and a date that suits.

  2. 02 We plan it together

    We reply within 24h on business days to confirm dates, logistics, and anything you want covered.

  3. 03 Your students play, learn & keep the pack

    Games, not lectures — on your premises, free of cost.

Aligned with CBSE's Cyber Clubs circular (Acad-101/2023), which invites schools to bring in external cyber-security experts for hands-on sessions.

We'll respond within 24h on business days. Final scheduling is confirmed by our team — submitting this form is a request, not a booking.
