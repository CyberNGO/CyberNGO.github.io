How it works

A simple process to get your club connected.

  1. 01

    You register

    Submit basic information about your club and its activities.

  2. 02

    We review

    A coordinator reviews your submission and may reach out for clarity.

  3. 03

    We connect

    Once approved, your club gets access to resources, events and community.

  4. 04

    You grow

    Collaborate, compete and grow together across the CyberNGO network.

What you gain as a member

Everything you need to build and scale impact.

  • CTF competitions Regular challenges and leaderboards
  • Mentorship network Guidance from industry experts
  • Resource library Curated tools, roadmaps and writeups
  • Events & workshops Host and join amazing events
  • Community channels Private Discord channels and announcements
  • Project showcase GitHub org and portfolio opportunities
  • Recognition Earn badges and featured spotlights
  • Inter-university challenges Compete with clubs across campuses

About Our NGO

CyberNGO helps people fight against Cyber Crime and understand the pain and concern of Cyber-crime victims. We help and guide people to fight against Cyber Crime — because being a victim of crime and fighting against it is not easy.
